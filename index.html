Bookshelf

Your library

Function: one place for every book you own, are reading or want to buy, so you can check in a second whether you already have it.

Usage: type any part of a title, author or category in the search. Tap a book to open it, tap an author to see all their books. Press N for a new book and / to search. Tap Connect Google Drive once to keep your books in your own Drive and see them on every device.
